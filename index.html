Games Rebirth

Old games from the 2000s, mostly mobile Java (J2ME) and PC titles, rebuilt from scratch so they can be played on today's devices.

Projects on GitHub

How we remake games

  • New code

    Every project is written from scratch. We do not copy or decompile the original game's code.

  • Own assets

    Art, sound and music are made by contributors or come under an open license, with credit.

  • Bring your own copy

    When a project needs the original data, players supply their own legally obtained copy. We never host game files.

  • Non-commercial

    No sales, no ads, and no donations tied to a remake.

Rights holders

If you own a game we work on and want anything removed, open a takedown request and it will be removed promptly, without argument. Read the full copyright and trademark notice.

Games Rebirth is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original developers, publishers or rights holders. All trademarks and copyrights belong to their owners.

Copyright and trademark notice

Games Rebirth is a non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to any original developer, publisher or rights holder of the games it remakes.

Trademarks and copyrights. Game titles, character names, logos and other trademarks belong to their respective owners. They are named here only to identify the original work a project is about. All rights to the original games remain with their owners.

What we do not distribute. Our repositories aim not to host or link to original game code, decompiled code, game files, ROMs, JARs or ripped art, sound and music. Projects are written from scratch, use assets made by contributors or under open licenses, and ask players to supply their own legally obtained copy where the original data is needed.

No money involved. We do not sell remakes, show ads or take donations tied to a remake.

Removal on request. If you own the rights to a game, or act for the owner, and want any content removed, we will remove it promptly, without argument. Open a takedown request (public), or use GitHub's private DMCA process.

These are our goals, not a guarantee that every file is free of third-party material. If you find something that should not be here, please tell us.
